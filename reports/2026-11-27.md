- **English**：**NDA draft rules on data for AI training (2026-11-26):** The National Data Administration opened consultation, until 6 Dec, on a 12-point draft opinion on data rules for AI. The draft says holders of data-use rights may use lawfully obtained data for AI training and inference, and holders of data-operating rights may supply it to others for the same purpose. It extends this to self-collected, derived and contractually acquired data, encourages paying for high-quality data and building industry datasets, and proposes sector-specific data-quality certification. It also asks AI providers to set up output-risk controls and complaint channels. The draft clarifies the legal path for training data, which is a modest positive for China AI-application names such as Xiaomi and Insilico. It is still a consultation draft with no funding, procurement or company-specific provisions.
