- **English**：**China's rules on decentralised digital identity (2026-11-27):** The Cyberspace Administration and ten other agencies, including MIIT, the PBOC and Customs, issued 44-article rules for a state-run decentralised digital-identity system built on the national blockchain network. The rules take effect on 1 March 2027. The identity chain will be run by an entity the CAC authorises; the rules do not name it. They set mandatory duties for operators and service providers, such as 24-hour emergency response, commercial-cryptography plans and limits on linking identities to other account data. They also encourage use in finance, tax, transport, customs and digital-yuan payments, and, notably, as identity tags for AI agents. There is no funding, procurement or named beneficiary, so this is plumbing for China's digital-government and AI-agent push rather than a demand driver for any portfolio name.
